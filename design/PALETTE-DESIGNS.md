# Palette designs (exploration, 2026-11-03)

Fifteen directions for the palette, rendered from the app in snapshot mode
(`design-01` … `design-15`, light and dark) over the same staged desktop: a window
dragged to the top, the right third of Columns × 3 under the pointer, and the drop
outline on the display. Each design reads only its own light and dark colors.
The one picked becomes the palette, with its own spec, tokens and states (design-kit).
